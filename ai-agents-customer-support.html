AI Agents & Customer Support

Help customers and staff find approved information, complete routine steps, and reach the right person when judgment or a sensitive situation requires it.

Where it can help

Useful when routine questions compete with work that needs staff expertise.

The goal is not to remove people. It is to create clearer assistance and escalation.

  • 01

    Customers repeatedly ask questions answered in approved business material.

  • 02

    Staff spend time searching across documents or internal resources.

  • 03

    Requests need consistent intake before they reach a person.

  • 04

    Escalation rules are unclear or applied inconsistently.

What this can include

Assistance grounded in approved information and defined actions.

Capabilities depend on the knowledge sources, channels, systems, and risk level involved.

01

Knowledge assistance

Answer eligible questions using selected, maintained business information.

02

Request intake

Collect required details and categorize routine requests.

03

Connected actions

Prepare tasks, searches, or record updates when permissions allow.

04

Staff escalation

Route low-confidence, complex, sensitive, or out-of-scope cases to people.

Illustrative conversation

A routine answer with a clear route to staff.

The actual topics, channels, and actions are agreed for each project.

How we build it

Start with eligible questions and explicit boundaries.

Testing includes expected questions, difficult phrasing, missing information, and escalation cases.

  1. 01

    Define

    Agree audiences, topics, sources, actions, and exclusions.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Organize approved knowledge and escalation rules.

  3. 03

    Test

    Evaluate representative questions and edge cases.

  4. 04

    Support

    Monitor, maintain sources, and improve agreed behavior.

Trust and oversight

People should know when they are interacting with AI.

We define disclosure, source boundaries, confidence handling, prohibited topics, staff escalation, and which actions require review.

Knowledge needs ownership and maintenance. An agent should not be treated as authoritative outside the information and behavior agreed for it.

Agent questions

Before building an AI assistant.

Will the agent answer every question?

No. Eligible topics, information sources, confidence rules, and escalation behavior are defined for the project.

Where does its information come from?

From the approved sources selected and prepared for the implementation. Source quality and maintenance affect behavior.

Can a person take over?

Handoff paths can be designed for complex, sensitive, low-confidence, or specifically excluded requests.

Support boundaries

Operational responsibility needs to be explicit.

Support scope, service windows, response targets, monitoring, hosting, vendor dependencies, security responsibilities, and change approval are agreed for the implementation.

Managed support does not remove the client’s ownership of business policy, risk decisions, source information, or staff responsibilities.

Support questions

Before agreeing an ongoing plan.

What is covered by managed support?

Coverage is defined for each implementation and may include monitoring, maintenance, updates, documentation, staff support, and agreed improvements.

Do you support systems built by others?

Potentially, after a technical and operational assessment establishes the system condition, documentation, access, dependencies, and feasible responsibilities.

Are response times fixed?

Response targets and service windows are defined in the agreed support terms; they are not assumed to be the same for every implementation.

Plan for ongoing use

What does your automation need after launch?

We’ll discuss the implementation, dependencies, owners, current issues, and level of support required.

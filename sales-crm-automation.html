CRM questions

Before connecting the sales process.

Do we need to replace our CRM?

Not necessarily. We first assess the capabilities, APIs, permissions, and limitations of the systems you already use.

Can follow-up messages be sent automatically?

Possible behavior depends on the use case, channel, consent requirements, and your approval rules. Human review can remain part of the process.

What about duplicate or incomplete records?

Those cases should be defined during workflow design, including when to flag a record for staff review.

Personal information is information that identifies, relates to, or can reasonably be connected with an individual. Please review this policy before submitting information or using website features that collect it.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with Reach First, we may request or receive:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, company, and other contact details.
  • Information submitted through consultation, contact, newsletter, feedback, support, account, checkout, or live-chat experiences.
  • Project, service, communication, order, billing, or account information needed to respond to you or provide a requested service.
  • Technical and usage information such as browser type, device details, IP address, pages visited, referral information, and interactions with the website or advertising.

When collection occurs

Information may be collected when you submit a form, communicate with us, request or purchase a service, create or use an account, subscribe, provide feedback, use a website feature, or otherwise provide information.

2. How we use information

Reach First may use collected information to:

  • Respond to enquiries, provide support, and follow up after email, telephone, form, or chat correspondence.
  • Provide, administer, personalize, and improve website experiences, services, content, and offers.
  • Process authorized orders and payments and maintain related business records.
  • Operate surveys, promotions, newsletters, advertising, analytics, or other website features.
  • Understand website traffic, preferences, and interactions so we can improve performance and usability.
  • Protect our users, business, systems, rights, property, and safety and comply with legal obligations.

3. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information. These may include restricted access, confidentiality requirements, secured networks, encryption in transit, vulnerability review, malware scanning, and other security controls appropriate to the information and service.

Where payments are processed through a payment gateway, payment information is handled by the applicable provider rather than stored or processed directly on Reach First servers, except for records needed to administer the transaction.

No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a qualifying incident occurs, Reach First will provide notifications as required by applicable law and will take reasonable responsive steps.

4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to recognize a browser, remember preferences, support requested features, understand traffic and interactions, measure advertising, and improve future website experiences.

Reach First may work with service providers, including Google services, that use first-party or third-party cookies and identifiers for analytics, advertising, remarketing, demographics, interest reporting, or measurement of ad interactions.

Managing cookies

You can use your browser settings to receive cookie warnings, restrict cookies, or disable them. Some features may work less efficiently when cookies are disabled. Advertising and analytics preferences may also be managed through applicable provider controls, including Google Ads settings and browser opt-out tools.

5. Sharing, service providers, and legal disclosure

Reach First does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with hosting, payment, analytics, advertising, communications, technical, professional, or other service providers that help operate the website or business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and use restrictions.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a lawful request, enforce policies or agreements, prevent fraud or security harm, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Reach First, users, or others.

Third-party services and links

External platforms and services are governed by their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information directly to them.

6. Your privacy choices

Subject to applicable law, you may contact Reach First to request access to or correction of personal information, update contact details, change communication preferences, or ask how information is handled.

You may browse publicly available areas of the website without creating an account. Where a browser sends a recognized privacy or Do Not Track signal, it will be handled according to applicable requirements and the capabilities of the website and its service providers.

Policy changes

Changes will be posted on this page. The revised version will apply from its published effective date unless otherwise stated.

7. Children and commercial email

Children’s privacy

Reach First does not specifically market its services to children under 13 and does not knowingly seek personal information from them through this business website.

Email communications

Commercial messages will be managed in accordance with applicable requirements. Reach First does not intend to use deceptive sender information or subject lines, and recipients may use an unsubscribe link or contact us to request that marketing email stop. Service or transactional communications may continue where required.

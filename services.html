Before we begin

Questions about choosing a service.

Not sure which category fits? That is a normal place to start.

Which service should we start with?

If the opportunity is still unclear, start with consulting and planning. If the priority is visibility or customer acquisition, start with digital marketing. A known operational bottleneck can be assessed through the relevant automation or integration service.

Can these services work with our existing software?

Potentially. We review available APIs, permissions, data access, marketing platforms, and project requirements before recommending how tools should connect.

Do we need a large engagement?

No. A focused workflow, campaign, channel, or customer journey is often the most useful place to begin and evaluate whether broader work is worthwhile.

How are cost and timing determined?

They depend on scope, systems, channels, research, creative requirements, data readiness, testing, and ongoing support. We clarify those factors before proposing the work.

What happens after launch?

We review agreed indicators and next steps. Automation maintenance or ongoing marketing execution and improvement can be included in the engagement.

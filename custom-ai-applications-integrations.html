Custom-build questions

Before designing the application.

How do we know custom development is necessary?

We first assess the workflow and existing-platform options. Custom work is useful when required behavior, interfaces, or connections cannot be achieved appropriately through configuration.

Can it connect to legacy software?

Possibly. Feasibility depends on available APIs, export/import options, permissions, documentation, and vendor constraints.

Who maintains the solution?

Ownership, hosting, monitoring, updates, documentation, and support responsibilities are agreed during scoping.
